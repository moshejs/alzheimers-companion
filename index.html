Alzheimer's Companion

Alzheimer's disease is the most common cause of dementia. Here it is in moving pictures: watch a protein get cut the wrong way, follow tangles spreading through the brain, see the changes that begin many years before the first forgotten name, and look honestly at what the newest drugs do and do not achieve.

Schematic. Amyloid rises first, tau follows, and memory changes come last.

Sources

Every reference returned by the nine research passes behind this app: journal articles, guidelines, drug labels, trial registrations and a few database or search records. The small numbers throughout the app point here.

Research for each area was done separately, so a few works appear under a merged entry. Entries marked check could not be fully verified against the original, so treat them with extra care.

    Alzheimer's Companion is an educational synthesis of the published literature on Alzheimer's disease, made for health-science students, people living with the disease, and the families who care for them.

    It is not medical advice. It cannot diagnose, and nothing here should change a treatment without your own clinician. Guideline doses are quoted as published. Please check the original sources, which are listed in full in the Sources module.
